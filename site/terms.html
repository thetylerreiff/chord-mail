02Your mail, your actions

Chord acts on your real Gmail account. When you archive, label, trash, snooze, schedule, or send, Chord makes that change in Gmail. Single keys do a lot, so read what you’re about to send, and use z to undo while you still can.

Chord works on a local copy of your mail and syncs it with Gmail in the background. Your mail stays in Gmail, but Chord isn’t a backup of it.
