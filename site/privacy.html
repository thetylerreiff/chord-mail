Privacy.

Chord has no servers. Your mail goes from Google to your computer and stays there. Nobody behind Chord, including its author, can see your mail, your contacts, or your account.

01What this covers

This policy covers Chord, the open-source Gmail client for Omarchy (“the app”), its installer, and this website, chord.thetylerreiff.co. Chord is made by Tyler Reiff. It is an independent project, not affiliated with Google or Omarchy.

02Your Google account

Chord connects to Gmail through an OAuth client that you create in your own Google Cloud project. Google issues your sign-in to that client, on your computer. It is never sent to Chord’s author or to anyone else.

Chord asks Google for one permission, the gmail.modify scope. It lets Chord read your mail and labels, and label, archive, trash, and send mail on your behalf. It can’t permanently delete mail, and it can’t touch anything in your Google account outside Gmail.

Chord uses this access only to show you your mail and to carry out the actions you take. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Chord never transfers your Google data to anyone, never uses it for advertising, and never sells it.

03Where the app connects

The app makes network requests only to Google: accounts.google.com and oauth2.googleapis.com to sign you in, and gmail.googleapis.com to sync your mail and carry out your actions. It has no telemetry, crash reporting, analytics, or update checks, and there is no Chord server for it to report to.

Google’s handling of your mail is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

04What stays on your computer

Everything Chord stores is in your home directory:

PathContents
~/.config/chord-mail/client_secret.jsonYour OAuth client
~/.config/chord-mail/config.tomlSettings
~/.local/share/chord-mail/token.jsonYour sign-in, readable only by you
~/.local/share/chord-mail/mail.dbA copy of your mail, the action queue, snoozes, and scheduled sends
~/.cache/chord-mail/drafts/Drafts while Neovim has them open

The background service shows desktop notifications for new Primary mail. They are drawn by your desktop and go no further.

05Images in mail

Chord blocks remote images, fonts, and other remote content in messages by default, so senders can’t use tracking pixels to learn when you open their mail. If you choose to load images for a message or always for a sender, your computer fetches them from the sender’s servers, and those servers can then see your IP address and that you opened the message.

06The installer

The install command downloads a script from this website. The script installs build tools from your Arch package mirrors, downloads Chord’s source from GitHub, and builds it, which downloads Rust libraries from crates.io. Those services see the requests, as with any download. The script sends nothing about you or your machine anywhere. You can read it before you run it.

07This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. The demo inbox and keyboard run entirely in your browser, and nothing you press is sent anywhere.

  • The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard request logs, such as IP address, browser, and page requested, to serve and protect the site.
  • Its fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser also sends Google a request for them.

08Removing your data

  • chord-mail logout deletes your saved sign-in.
  • To remove everything, also delete the three chord-mail directories listed above.
  • To revoke Chord’s access from Google’s side, remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because Chord’s author never receives your data, there is nothing to delete on our end.

09Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date, and every change is visible in the project’s history. Questions go to the issue tracker.
